


# ![writeonce-logo-f260d4d6](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dcd8ec42-4519-4e89-80bd-ca42f135385d)  WriteOnce

**Generate professional client documents in minutes, not hours.**

WriteOnce is an open source tool for consultants, agencies, and freelancers who need to create proposals, contracts, statements of work, and invoices quickly and consistently.

Instead of rewriting or copy-pasting old documents, WriteOnce lets you:

* ✍️ **Build once**: Paste your existing proposal or contract.
* 🪄 **Mark variables**: Highlight details like client name, fees, or dates.
* ⚡ **Reuse forever**: Save the template and reuse it for every client.
* 📄 **Export instantly**: Fill in client details and download a polished PDF.

### Why Use WriteOnce?

* 🚀 Close deals faster by turning around proposals in minutes.
* ✅ Eliminate copy-paste mistakes (no more wrong client names!).
* 💼 Keep all your client documents consistent and professional.
* 💸 Save hours each week that you can spend on billable work.

### Who It’s For

* Independent consultants
* Creative and marketing agencies
* Freelancers
* Professional service providers
